## 09. Epistemic Context Learning Teaches AI Agents Which of Their Peers to Trust
**Category:** research | **Read time:** 5 min

## 11. LLMs Can Reason Deep. A New Benchmark Asks Whether They Can Reason Wide.
**Category:** research | **Read time:** 5 min
MPAR-Bench, a 1,000-item English-Chinese benchmark built on the party game Just One, tests whether models can converge several unrelated clues onto one hidden word.
